Policy Brief:  Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna
Yojana, Madhya Pradesh

Mr. Anil kr dubey

Project Consultant, Freelancer

Abstract
“Mukhyamantri  Ladli  Behna  Yojana  (Ladli  Bahna)”  is  a  large-scale  unconditional  cash-transfer
programme for adult women in Madhya Pradesh, launched in 28 January 2023 and expanded since then.
It provides monthly DBT payments (recently raised – Oct 2025 to Rs 1,500 per beneficiary) to eligible
women  to  improve  household  welfare,  nutrition  and  women‟s  bargaining  power.  The  programme
reaches  over  1.2  crore  beneficiaries  and  now  represents  a  major  recurring  fiscal  commitment  (≈Rs
22,000  crore/year  after  the  latest  increase).  By emphasising its  focus  on  women  from  the  low-income
groups in both rural and urban areas, the Scheme has taken steps towards accomplishing the Sustainable
Development Goals (SDGs), particularly on gender equality, poverty alleviation & financial inclusion.

Background & Objectives-
Women-  particularly  from  the  marginalised  sections  of  the  society-  face  severe  economic  challenges,
primarily due to their limited access to resources, brim employment prospects and weak social support
networks. Factoring in their reliance on the male counterpart for financial support makes them the most
vulnerable, as it restricts their capacity to make financial decisions on their own. The “Ladli Behana”
Scheme was introduced  in response to these persistent issues.  By  directly  assisting  women  financially
and encouraging their financial independence, consequently enhancing their social status. The Scheme
aims to address gender based economic disparity.
The  scheme  provides  regular  cash  assistance  to  women  (married,  widowed,  divorced,  abandoned,  and
certain frontline female workers) via Direct Benefit Transfer into Aadhaar- linked bank accounts. It was
originally introduced at Rs.1,000/month in 2023 and progressively increased. The main objectives are to
improve women‟s economic autonomy, household nutrition, and strengthen women‟s social status and
decision-making power within families.

Key Features of the Scheme-
1.  Women belonging to the Schedule Tribes (STs), Scheduled Castes (SCs) and other backward castes
(OBCs)– groups that are prone to social and economic disadvantages – are among the marginalised
communities and are given more priority under the Scheme.

2.  The  Scheme  incorporates  Financial  Literacy  Training  to  improve  the  impact  of  the  financial  aid
provided.  These  initiatives  enable  the  recipients  to  prudently  handle  their  finances  and  encourage
well-informed spending and saving choices.

3.  Monthly  cash  assistance:  Rs.1,500  per  eligible  woman  (revised  from  Rs.1,250  from  November

2025).

•  Beneficiaries:- Approximately 1.26–1.27 crore women across all districts of Madhya Pradesh.

Implementation Process-
The scheme operates through an extensive digital and administrative infrastructure:
1.  Application and verification through local gram panchayats and urban bodies MunicipalCorprations .
2.  Beneficiary list validation using Aadhaar and bank linkage.
3.  Direct credit of funds into beneficiaries‟ accounts on pre-announced dates.
4.  Monitoring via district-level officers and online MIS dashboards.

Impact Analysis-
Preliminary  assessments  and  survey  data  indicate  that  the  “Ladli  Behna  Yojana”  has  increased
household  consumption,  improved  women‟s  control  over  expenditure,  and  enhanced  their  sense  of
economic  security.  According  to  SBI  Research  (2024),  beneficiary  households  reported  an  average
monthly spending increase of Rs.9,300.
The scheme has also contributed to financial inclusion by promoting bank account usage among women.

Key Challenges-
1.  Fiscal sustainability: The recurring expenditure places a heavy burden on the state budget.
2.  Targeting inefficiencies: Risk of inclusion of ineligible beneficiaries and exclusion of eligible poor

women.

3.  Administrative capacity: Ensuring real-time verification and grievance handling at scale.
4.  Lack of complementary skill or livelihood programmes linked to the cash transfers.

Policy Recommendations-
1.  Develop a 3-year fiscal sustainability roadmap to manage financial burden.
2.  Introduce periodic verification and social audits to maintain beneficiary integrity.
3.  Integrate cash transfers with optional skill development, microfinance, and livelihood programmes.
4.  Establish a transparent, real-time data dashboard for public accountability.
5.  Commission independent impact evaluations to assess long-term socio-economic outcomes.

Conclusion;-
The  “Mukhyamantri  Ladli  Behna  Yojana”  has  emerged  as  a  transformative  welfare  programme  for
women  in  Madhya  Pradesh.  While  its  scale  and  coverage  have  provided  significant  socio-economic
benefits,  the  sustainability  and  efficiency  of  the  scheme  depend  on  improved  fiscal  planning,  better
targeting, and integration with broader empowerment initiatives. With the right reforms, the scheme can
serve as a model for gender-responsive governance across India.
